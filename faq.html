FAQ

The questions
worth asking.

Six honest answers about field work, our service areas, and what to expect on site.

01

What does IntegrateXpress do on site?

+
We assess facilities, build out telecom closets, run structured cabling, install alarms and security cameras, and fix what breaks after go-live. That is field infrastructure and security work, not software development. ERP consulting and payroll integration through ADP or Paychex live on their own pages when that is the work you need.
02

How is infrastructure different from ERP consulting?

+
Infrastructure is physical work in your building: surveys, cabling, closets, cameras, and alarms. ERP consulting covers selection, implementation, and adoption for the business systems you already run. Each type of work has its own page and form so everyone knows what is in scope.
03

What happens during an infrastructure assessment?

+
We walk the site, photograph pathways and telecom spaces, note power and cooling, and map surveillance and alarm needs. You receive a photo-documented report with scope, timeline, and next steps, usually within one to two weeks of the visit.
04

Where do you provide field services?

+
Our New York service areas include Rochester, Niagara Falls, Buffalo, Syracuse, Albany, and the Capital of New York. On the Contact page, tap a star on the map to jump to each location.
05

What are the benefits of hiring IntegrateXpress for field work?

+
You get experienced field help without adding headcount: documented assessments, tested installs, and go-live support when something fails. We coordinate with your facilities team and vendors so the job finishes on scope.
06

What complications can arise during a project?

+
Common issues include hidden pathway constraints, outdated cabling, access scheduling, vendor coordination, and timeline pressure. For ERP or payroll work, process alignment and training add complexity. We help you anticipate these during assessment and planning.
