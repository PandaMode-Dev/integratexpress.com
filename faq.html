FAQ

The questions
worth asking.

Four honest answers about field work, our service areas, and what to expect on site.

01

What does IntegrateXpress do on site?

+
We assess facilities, build out telecom closets, run structured cabling, install alarms and security cameras, and fix what breaks after go-live.
02

What happens during an infrastructure assessment?

+
We walk the site, photograph pathways and telecom spaces, note power and cooling, and map surveillance and alarm needs. You receive a photo-documented report with scope, timeline, and next steps, usually within one to two weeks of the visit.
03

Where do you provide field services?

+
Our New York service areas include Rochester, Niagara Falls, Buffalo, Syracuse, and the Capital of New York.
04

What are the benefits of hiring IntegrateXpress for field work?

+
You get experienced field help without adding headcount: documented assessments, tested installs, and go-live support when something fails. We coordinate with your facilities team and vendors so the job finishes on scope.
