What we deliver

Infrastructure assessments
& network build-outs

From site surveys through equipment rollouts, we help you plan, build, and maintain reliable telecommunications and network infrastructure.

Site survey

Conducting a thorough examination of a site to gather data and assess its suitability for telecommunication installations. This involves evaluating factors such as terrain, existing infrastructure, and environmental conditions.

T1 extensions & relocation

Extending or relocating T1 lines: dedicated digital communication links commonly used for data, voice, and video transmissions.

Turnkey commercial relocation

Comprehensive services for the relocation of telecommunication equipment and systems in commercial settings, including disconnecting, moving, and reinstalling the necessary infrastructure.

Telecom room build-outs & renovation

Designing and constructing telecommunication rooms or data centers within a building, including power, cooling, cabling, and equipment racks.

Phone system relocation & VoIP cabling

Moving and reconfiguring phone systems, including Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP), and installing the cabling infrastructure required for phone networks.

Equipment rollout documentation & testing

Documenting and testing existing telecommunication infrastructure to ensure readiness for new equipment or systems, including connectivity, performance, and compatibility.

Regional servicing for national vendor accounts

Telecommunications services and support for national vendor accounts in specific regions, including maintenance, troubleshooting, and smooth day-to-day operation.

Network routers & switches

Installing and configuring network routers and switches for routing data and managing traffic across your network.

TV cable outlets

Installing cable outlets for television distribution and connection to cable service providers.

Wireless & wired access points

Deploying wireless and wired access points so devices can connect reliably across your space.

Surveillance & CCTV

Installing closed-circuit television cameras for video surveillance and security, including strategic placement and integration into your security infrastructure.

Security DVR & data cabling

Configuring digital video recorders for surveillance footage and installing data and computer cabling to connect DVRs to the network.
