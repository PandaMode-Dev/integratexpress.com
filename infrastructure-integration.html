Infrastructure assessments

Know your site before you build.

We walk your facility, photograph pathways and telecom spaces, and map what exists today — power, cooling, cable routes, alarm coverage, and camera sight lines. You get a practical scope document your facilities team can act on, usually within one to two weeks of the site visit.

  • Photo-documented walkthroughs & pathway maps
  • Closet and telecom room readiness
  • Surveillance and alarm scope
  • Rollout, relocation, and fix-service timelines
